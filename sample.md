## 01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

I'm a student, maker, and aspiring computer engineer fascinated by the intersection of hardware and software.

I enjoy understanding things from the ground up — from the electrons moving through a circuit to the software that gives a machine its purpose.

My work spans robotics, embedded systems, PCB design, software development, and entrepreneurship. I particularly enjoy projects that cross disciplinary boundaries.

**What drives me isn't simply making things work. It's making things that matter.**
